Shared rules

The family resemblance.

Same skeleton

8px cards, round status chips, restrained borders, mono numerals, tight table rows, and dense but calm information hierarchy.

Same signals

Green means healthy, live, approved, or ready. Gold means commercial value. Blue means evidence, audit, source, or verification.

Different lighting

CORE inverts the surface for deep work. STACK stays light because SMB users need inspection, trust, and shareable proof.
