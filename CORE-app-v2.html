What CORE is

The Dev's app, end to end.

CORE is the app the Dev signs in to. STACK is the AI tooling that ships at the SMB. This deck walks CORE only. CORE has four working areas: Practice for the whole book of clients, then Sell, Deploy, and Operate for the lifecycle of any one client.

The Dev's whole book of clients in one view. Where the practice lives across every deployment.

Anchor scenario

Acme Legal — 25-person firm in Brisbane.

A growing commercial-litigation practice. Document handover takes days, junior staff burn hours classifying and chasing missing pieces, and partners want partners' time back.

Vertical

Document Intake STACK

Selected because the firm's pain is concentrated in document handovers and matter setup.

Tier

Growth, ~1,800 docs/mo

Activation $1,500. STACK $999/mo. Headroom for matter intake spikes.

Dev

Dev McLean, on Starter CORE

Solo consultant, ex-platform engineer. First paid Document Intake deployment.

Sprint

3-week scoped engagement

Discovery, configure, eval, train, go-live. Followed by an optional ongoing retainer.
