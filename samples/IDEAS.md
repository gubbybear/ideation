### Alex — 2026-04-10

**Idea:** WhatsApp bot for receipt capture

Instead of building a native app, what if users just texted photos of receipts to a WhatsApp number? We process them server-side. Zero friction for onboarding — no app download needed. Could be our wedge to get users before building a full app.

### Alex — 2026-04-10

**Idea:** "Tax countdown" gamification

Show users a live countdown to their tax deadline with a readiness score. "You're 73% ready for April 5th." Creates urgency and makes the boring task feel manageable. Could drive daily engagement.

### Jordan — 2026-04-11

**Idea:** Receipt email alias

Give each user a unique email like `alex-7xk@receipts.app`. They forward or auto-forward purchase confirmations. We parse them. Works for online purchases where there's no physical receipt. Paired with the photo capture, this covers most scenarios.

### Jordan — 2026-04-11

**Idea:** "Accountant mode" — share a read-only view

Freelancers who have an accountant could share a link. Accountant sees categorized expenses, downloads reports. We become the connective tissue between freelancer chaos and accountant order. Could be a wedge into the accountant channel.

### Alex — 2026-04-12

**Idea:** Bank statement screenshot parser

Some users won't connect Open Banking (trust issues). Let them screenshot their bank app and we OCR the transactions. Fallback for the privacy-conscious. Not as clean but removes a major onboarding blocker.
