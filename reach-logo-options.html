Reach logo exploration

Three parent-brand directions.

Each option treats Reach as the umbrella brand, with CORE and STACK as sub-brands. The shared idea is prehensile reach: a flexible extension that finds, grips, connects, and pulls useful work into range.

Option 01

The Curl

A custom R-mark where the leg becomes a reaching curl. This is the closest to a prehensile nose or tail: soft enough to feel dexterous, structured enough for B2B trust.

Organic Memorable Best icon
  • Strongest standalone app icon.
  • CORE and STACK can use the same mark with dark/light mode switching.
  • Risk: friendlier and more expressive than the current austere deck.

Option 02

The Grasp

A reaching loop curls around a node. This option turns the prehensile idea into a product metaphor: identify, wrap, hold, and connect. It is less letter-like and more ownable as a symbol.

Network Matchmaking Most distinctive
  • Best fit if Reach Market becomes central to the story.
  • Feels like matching, routing, and controlled handoff.
  • Risk: less obvious as an R unless paired with the wordmark.

Option 03

The Hookline

A precise hook reaches out from the R and returns as an underline. This is the most enterprise-facing direction: less creature-like, more tool-like, with the prehensile idea expressed as controlled extension.

Enterprise Precise Best wordmark
  • Feels closest to a premium SaaS identity.
  • Very clean CORE / STACK sub-brand system.
  • Risk: less emotional and less surprising than the other two.
