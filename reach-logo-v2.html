Construction

Three geometric primitives, one path

Anatomy

Drawn as a single SVG path with even-odd fill so the bowl's counter shows whatever sits behind — works on any surface without colour management. Same stroke weight (28u on a 200×220 grid) on stem, bowl, and leg keeps the mark structurally consistent.

1 Stem · the foundation28u wide vertical, full height. Anchors the practice.
2 Bowl · the turn50u outer radius, 22u inner. Slightly wider than the stem for visual balance.
3 Leg · the descentSquare-cut tip lands flush at the baseline. Same 28u thickness as the stem.
